# LinkLoom launch material

## Community post

Title: Share a cleaner link in one click.

I built LinkLoom, a Chrome extension for a simpler everyday browsing workflow.

LinkLoom removes recognized tracking parameters from URLs before you share them. Open the extension on a web page, review the cleaned link, and copy it into a chat, email, or document.

What it does:
- Preview the current page’s clean URL
- Remove recognized tracking parameters
- Clean up to 100 pasted links at once

Install: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/pkodiocfdnhpbpmjdjlidbhedfkdjnnh

I am the developer. I would appreciate feedback on the workflow, confusing steps, or missing essentials. Please share your own experience; there is no reward or required rating.
